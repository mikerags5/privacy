Getting started

  1. Add the things you look after: your house, cars, a boat, appliances. Guided setup (Settings → Set Up Items) adds a house and vehicles in a few taps.
  2. Put the parts inside each one: the furnace and water heater in the house, the engine and trailer in the boat.
  3. Log what you do: an oil change, a filter, a repair, with the date, the cost and the mileage or hours. Add photos, receipts and scanned manuals.
  4. Set reminders by date (every year) or by mileage and hours (every 5,000 miles). Home shows what's overdue and what's coming up.

Want to look around first? Choose Explore with Sample Data at the end of the tutorial. Clear Sample Data… on Home removes it again and leaves anything of your own alone.

Common questions

My reminders don't send notifications. Open Settings → Notifications in Track It. If permission says Denied, tap Open iOS Settings and turn on Allow Notifications. Also check that Mute all notifications is off and that quiet hours aren't covering the time you expect.

How do reminders by mileage or hours work? Give the item a unit (miles, kilometers or hours) and log readings as you go, either with an event or with Update under the item's name. When a reading passes the reminder's mark, it's due and you get a notification.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone? Your normal iPhone backup includes Track It. You can also use Settings → Export Full Backup to save a ZIP file, then Settings → Restore from Backup on the new phone.

Does it sync between devices? No. Everything is stored on your iPhone, and nothing is sent anywhere.

How do I add the widget? Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Track It.

Can I give a buyer the service history? Yes. Open the item, tap ⋯, and choose Export Service History (PDF). It covers the item and everything inside it.
